PetMate — Child Safety Standards

PetMate is committed to protecting children and strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form on our platform.

Zero Tolerance Policy

PetMate explicitly prohibits any content, behavior, or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children, including but not limited to grooming, sextortion, or trafficking of minors. This applies to all users, profiles, messages, and any other content shared on PetMate.

Reporting Mechanism

PetMate provides an in-app reporting and blocking feature, accessible from any chat or profile, allowing users to report concerning content or behavior directly to us for review.

Our Response Process

Upon receiving a report or otherwise becoming aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM (child sexual abuse material) on our platform, PetMate will take immediate action, including but not limited to removing the content, suspending or terminating the responsible account, and reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement, in accordance with applicable laws.
