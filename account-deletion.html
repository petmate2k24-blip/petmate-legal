Delete Your PetMate Account

PetMate lets you permanently delete your account and associated data directly within the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the PetMate app and log in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email, photos)
  • Your pet profiles and their photos
  • Your matches, likes, and chat messages
  • Your adoption posts

Retention period

Your account and data are placed in a 30-day grace period before final, permanent deletion, in case you change your mind. During this period, your account is deactivated and hidden from other users. After 30 days, all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.
